# Code and Data Availability

## Submission-ready statement

**Code availability.** The analysis scripts, concept-level codebooks, statistical analysis plans, and non-identifying aggregate source data supporting the reported figures and summary tables are publicly available at https://github.com/FENG1567/us-brazil-biliary-procedure-completion-gaps (release v1.0.0). Repository-authored code is released under the MIT License. Scripts that depend on restricted third-party microdata can be executed only by users who have independently obtained the required access.

**Data availability.** The National Readmissions Database (NRD) is available through the Healthcare Cost and Utilization Project (HCUP) under a data-use agreement and cannot be redistributed by the authors. SIH-SUS and CNES data are available from DATASUS, IBGE data are available from the Brazilian Institute of Geography and Statistics, and OSRM routing data and services are available from their source organizations, subject to the respective source terms. The public submission package and repository contain no patient-level records. They include concept-level codebooks, protocols, aggregate summary tables, and non-identifying source data for the displayed figures. Fine-grained NRD hospital-year outputs containing exact small cells are not redistributed and must be regenerated by approved users within their secure environment. The MIT License applies only to repository-authored code and does not supersede third-party licences or data-use agreements.

## Scope notes

- The public files support inspection of reported aggregate results but do not replace the controlled source data.
- Complete re-execution requires independent access to the applicable NRD, SIH-SUS, CNES, IBGE, and routing inputs.
- No claim is made that MIT licensing applies to third-party data, source-agency records, or materials governed by separate publication terms.
